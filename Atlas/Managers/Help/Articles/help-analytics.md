1. Click **Ask Apple for Reports**. Apple needs a day or two to prepare them the first time.
2. Read the funnel: impressions, page views, downloads, and trials and paid starts for subscriptions.
3. Use **Break It Down** to compare countries and sources, and **What to Try** for changes worth making.

> To see trials, paid starts and proceeds, add your vendor number in Settings → Accounts. The key needs the Admin, Finance or Sales role.
