Reviews are shown in your language. **Show Original** shows what the person wrote.

A suggested reply comes with its meaning in your language underneath, so you know what you are sending.

- **Reply to All** answers every review about one subject, with one message per language.

> Replies are public. Apple shows a reply under the review, usually within a day, and you can edit it later.
