An event needs a name, two descriptions, dates, and two images: the event card (1920 × 1080) and the event details image (1080 × 1920).

## Make the artwork

1. Open the event and click **Make the Artwork…**
2. Check the brief Atlas wrote, and choose the look.
3. Choose one of three concepts. Each comes as the card and the details image together.
4. Check it under the App Store’s text, in any language, then click **Use This Artwork**.

> Leave words out of the art. The App Store writes the event’s name over the image in each language.
