| The icon | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Still | Nothing running, nothing waiting |
| A bright stretch going round | Atlas is working: a push, Autopilot, a check with Apple |
| A dot | Something needs you |
| Dimmed, with a pause mark | Background work is paused |
| A stroke across it | Atlas can’t reach Apple, or the key was refused |

Click the icon to see what is running and what needs you. To really quit, choose **Quit Atlas** or press ⌘Q.
