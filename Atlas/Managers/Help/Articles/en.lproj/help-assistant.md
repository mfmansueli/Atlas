1. Open Settings → AI Assistants and click **Connect** next to Claude Code, Claude for Mac or Cursor.
2. Drop your project folder on the sheet, or drag it from Xcode’s title bar (optional).
3. Atlas starts the assistant and shows its first message. Nothing to copy.

No answer in 30 seconds? The sheet shows likely fixes. Other assistants: copy the line under **Advanced** into it.

| An assistant can | It never can |
| --- | --- |
| Read your listings, prices and app information | Push anything to App Store Connect |
| Suggest text, prices, answers, replies and artwork | Accept a suggestion for you |
| Read your project to find evidence | See your .p8 key |
