It reads your apps from App Store Connect, shows each one the way people see it on the App Store, and points out what could be better in every language.

- **It suggests.** Suggestions come from your assistant and from Atlas’s own checks. They’re always purple.
- **You review.** Accept, edit or dismiss each one, or a whole language at once.
- **One push sends it.** Nothing reaches the App Store until you press Push in Review & Push.

> Atlas does not replace Xcode or TestFlight. Builds, testers and your conversation with App Review stay where they are.
