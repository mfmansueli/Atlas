A nomination tells Apple’s editorial team what’s coming: what it is, when it starts, where and on which devices. Apple suggests nominating a few weeks ahead. Submitting doesn’t mean it will be featured: Apple doesn’t say which nominations it picks.

## Write a nomination

1. Open **Nominations** (⇧⌘F) and click **New Nomination…**
2. Choose the type: **App Launch**, **App Enhancements** or **New Content**.
3. Write the name and description: what’s new, who it’s for and why now.
4. Set the start date, the devices, languages and countries.
5. Click **Add to Review & Push**. Nothing is sent until you push.

Atlas suggests the type and a description from the version’s What’s New and your In-App Events, and offers the app’s links for **Materials**. Each suggestion shows why; accept or dismiss it.

> Leave links out of the description: Apple refuses them there. Put a video, deck or mockups in **Materials** as links.

## Submit and archive

When the draft is ready, click **Submit…** and confirm. Submit waits in Review & Push as its own row; once it’s pushed, the nomination can’t be changed, only archived. **Archive…** removes a submitted nomination from the editors’ list.

## When something goes wrong

- **The key can’t create nominations**: use a key with the Admin or App Manager role. Click **Change Key…**
- **Apple refused it**: click **Details** to read why, fix it and add it to Review & Push again.
- **The start date has passed**: pick a new date before you add it.
- **Offline**: drafts on this Mac stay; Atlas reads App Store Connect again when you’re back online.
