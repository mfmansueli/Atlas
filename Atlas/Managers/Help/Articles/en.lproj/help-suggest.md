A purple number in the sidebar counts the suggestions waiting in that section.

Each suggestion says who made it and why, under the field it belongs to.

- **Accept** keeps it, **Edit** lets you change it first, **Dismiss** removes it.
- **Accept All Languages** shows every language first, so you can untick any.

> You accept prices, App Privacy answers (not its URLs), the age rating and App Compliance one by one.
