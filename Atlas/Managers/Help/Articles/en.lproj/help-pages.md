A custom product page has its own screenshots, App Previews and promotional text per language. Name, icon and description match the default page. Up to 70 pages per app.

## Make a page

1. Click **New Page…**, name it, and start from the default page, another page, or nothing.
2. Write its promotional text and choose its keywords in each language that needs its own.
3. Click **Submit for Review…**, push this page’s changes, then **Submit for Review**. No new app version needed.

Once Apple approves it, the page’s link works. Turn **Visible** on to show it for the keywords you chose; that needs no review.

Atlas tells you when a page is in review, approved or sent back. For notifications, turn on **App Review changes a version’s or an event’s status** in Settings → Notifications. The reviewer’s message is only in App Store Connect.

## Screenshots for a page

A page shows the default page’s screenshots until it has its own. To make them, click **Make Screenshots for This Page…** under the page’s screenshots (Atlas Pro).

1. Your assistant plans the set for the audience: screen order and captions per language. Adjust the angle; choose languages and devices.
      - **Look:** the default page’s template, **Another template…**, or **A new look from Claude Design**: pick one of three looks your assistant draws.
      - **Capture screens again:** your assistant captures up to 3 screens again that should differ, such as with the lock on. Each is marked “captured again”; **Use the Default** reverts one. Default captures don’t change.
2. Click **Start Autopilot**. You can close the window; the page shows progress.
3. Compare with the default set. **Ask for Changes…** redoes this page’s set only.
4. Click **Use These Screenshots**. They wait in Review & Push, one change per language and device, until you push and submit the page.

Have App Preview videos? Click **Choose App Previews…**: up to 3 MOV, MP4 or M4V files per language and device, 886 × 1920 (iPhone) or 1200 × 1600 (iPad), either way round, 15 to 30 seconds, up to 500 MB. They wait in Review & Push; the default page’s don’t change.

Have screenshots? Click **Choose Files…**: up to 10 PNG or JPEG images per language and device. iPhone: 1320 × 2868, 1290 × 2796 or 1260 × 2736. iPad: 2064 × 2752 or 2048 × 2732. Either way round. A file that doesn’t fit is named with the sizes that work, and nothing is added. Files that fit wait in Review & Push, replacing any earlier set for that language and device.

The default page’s screenshots, captions and template don’t change.

## Test the header or search image

Product Page Optimization tests another header or search image (the treatment) on some visitors, to see which brings more downloads. One test at a time.

1. In Review & Push, click **Test with Product Page Optimization…** beside the header or the search results artwork Autopilot made.
2. Click **Ask for the Outcome Variant**. Your assistant designs a second image with one idea changed, and Atlas checks it in every language.
3. Click **Create Test** to make a draft in App Store Connect. Start it there, or tick **Send it to App Review once it’s made**. If the image can’t attach, Atlas saves both and says what to do.

Your assistant can suggest a test: name, share of visitors, languages and why. It waits in Review & Push; **Review Test…** opens it filled in, and nothing is made until **Create Test**. **Dismiss** drops it. The treatment is the run’s variant or a library image. It can also suggest deleting a draft test in the way.

> Stop a running test and see its results (conversion and improvement) in App Store Connect. Atlas doesn’t show them.

## Where a page’s link is used

The list under a page’s numbers shows its top sources over 28 days: campaign links (`ct` token) or linking apps and websites. Hover for the rest. Source: Apple’s detailed reports, as in **Analytics → Break It Down → Referrer** and **Campaign**.

## If a push didn’t finish

If Apple answers too slowly, the page may exist anyway. Atlas never retries: after **Refresh**, the draft becomes that page, and what’s missing (keywords, texts, the deep link) waits in Review & Push.

## What promotional text can’t say

Apple rejects store text that talks about prices or offers, so Atlas stops an assistant’s text that does and shows it as **Not offered**, with the words marked.

- No prices, “free” in any language, discounts or trial lengths. The App Store shows the price and offers itself.
- No other platforms or app stores, such as Android or Google Play.
- No beta, demo or “coming soon”, and no placeholder text.
- Only what this version of the app really does.

> **Ask for Another** asks your assistant for a new text that follows these rules.

## Keywords

Keywords come from the last approved version, and each leads to one page. Picking one another page uses moves it here, after you confirm.
