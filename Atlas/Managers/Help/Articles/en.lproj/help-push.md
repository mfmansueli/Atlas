1. Click **Review & Push** at the top right. The number is how many changes are waiting.
2. Read the changes, grouped by section and language. Untick anything that should wait.
3. Click **Push**. Atlas sends the changes and shows each one as it lands.

If a push stops halfway, what already landed stays on the App Store and the rest stays in Review & Push.

Approved screenshots and art from Autopilot or a product page join this list (Approve All, or Use These Screenshots). They’re pushed only from here.

> Some changes go live at once, such as promotional text and prices. Others wait for your next version’s review. Review & Push marks which is which.
