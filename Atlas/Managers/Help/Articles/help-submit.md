1. On **Overview**, the version card lists what goes to Apple and what is missing.
2. Click **Submit for Review…** Choose the build, check What’s New in every language, and answer the encryption question.
3. Choose what happens after approval: release right away, by you, or on a date.

> The bottom of the sidebar shows the version’s status from every section. If Apple rejects it, the card shows Apple’s message in full and where to fix it.
