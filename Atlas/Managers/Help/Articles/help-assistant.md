1. Open Settings → AI Assistants and click **Connect** next to Claude Code, Claude for Mac or Cursor.
2. Drop your project’s folder on the sheet, or drag the project from the title of Xcode’s window. Atlas checks it holds your app. You can also connect without a project.
3. Atlas adds itself to the assistant, starts it, and shows its first message. There’s nothing to type or copy.

If the assistant doesn’t answer within 30 seconds, the sheet lists the usual reasons, each with a button: restart it and try again, open the assistant, or open this page. Any other assistant: open **Advanced** and copy the line to paste into it.

| An assistant can | It never can |
| --- | --- |
| Read your listings, prices and app information | Push anything to App Store Connect |
| Suggest text, prices, answers, replies and artwork | Accept a suggestion for you |
| Read your project to find evidence | See your .p8 key |
