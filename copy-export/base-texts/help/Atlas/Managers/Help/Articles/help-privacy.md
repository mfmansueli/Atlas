- The key is in the Keychain. Requests are signed here and go straight to Apple.
- An assistant gets only what it asks for through the local server: text, captures, prices. Never a key.
- With an API key, the text being suggested goes from this Mac to the provider you chose.
- Atlas’s developer receives nothing: Atlas has no analytics or crash reporting of its own.
