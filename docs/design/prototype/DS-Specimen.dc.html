What to build, and the rules behind it

Every colour, size, button and motion used in the clickable app, cleaned into one system. Where the prototype disagrees with itself, this board shows the decision. The README beside it explains why, lists every fix, and has the SwiftUI tokens.

1 · Colour has a job

Five meanings, never mixed: blue is what you do, purple is what AI suggests, green · amber · red say how things are. Neutrals carry everything else. The section colours (2) are only for recognising places.

4 · Buttons

Seven kinds, each with one job. The rule that matters most: one filled blue button per card or sheet. Repeated actions in a list (Accept, Send Reply) are tinted, so a page of suggestions doesn’t become a wall of blue. Purple never fills a button except when AI is waiting for you.

7 · AI motion

Motion belongs to the moments when an assistant does something: working, arriving, writing, being accepted. Everything else is still. Never more than one ambient effect on screen, nothing loops unless work is running, and every effect has a Reduce Motion version. The demos here loop so you can watch them; in the app each plays when its moment happens.
